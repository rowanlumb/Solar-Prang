Comments & Lessons

  • Generally should have learned electroplating process with much smaller test pieces first. This is a large complicated geometry for a new process and it was foolhardy to go big without doing reps doing smaller objects.
  • Should have rounded sharp corners and also chamfered sharp edges to stop local deposit maxima during the electroplating process
  • DIY Graphite paint was applied thickly to ensure low enough resistance to actually plate the object but many thin layers would be better for maintaining a smooth surface finish
  • Overall product would be vastly improved with better electroplating quality and by making it a warm light that turns on at night so the solar panels are doing useful work.
